About Premium Contractors

Trusted by Marriott, Hilton
and Wendy's

Premium Contractors, located in Newark, NJ, is a trusted name in the construction industry, known for delivering high-quality projects with precision and integrity. Specializing in building and renovating hotels, restaurants, and commercial spaces, we have partnered with renowned brands like Marriott, Hilton, and Wendy's.

With a commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction, we provide a wide range of services, from metal framing and drywall to finishing and cleaning. Backed by years of experience, Premium Contractors is dedicated to shaping spaces and exceeding client expectations across the region.
