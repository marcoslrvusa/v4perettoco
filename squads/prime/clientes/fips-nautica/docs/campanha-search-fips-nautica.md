## 2. Audience Segments

Apply at **campaign level as Observation** (não restringe impressões, mas permite ver performance e ajustar lances por segmento). Quando tiver 15-30 conversões, migre os que performarem para **Targeting** para estreitar o público.

### In-Market Segments

| Segment | Tipo | Bid Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Boats & Watercraft | In-Market | +20% |
| Boat Parts, Service & Repair | In-Market | +20% |
| Marine Equipment & Accessories | In-Market | +15% |
| Sporting Goods — Water Sports | In-Market | +10% |

### Custom Segments (Custom Intent)

Create these in Google Ads > Audience Manager > Custom Segments:

| Custom Segment Name | Keywords / URLs suggested |
|---|---|
| **Marine Repair Seekers** | marine repair, hydraulic repair, boat repair, boat mechanic, marine service, "needs boat repair", hydraulic hose, "boat not working", marine technician |
| **Boat Owners Miami FTL** | boat owner, my boat, marina Miami, Fort Lauderdale boating, yacht maintenance, boat maintenance, dock, slip |
| **DIY Boat Repair Lookup** (negative — bid -50%) | how to repair boat, fix boat myself, boat repair youtube, marine repair guide, boat repair cost, replace hydraulic hose, rebuild cylinder, bleed hydraulic system |

> 💡 **Por que o terceiro segment é negativo:** O diagnóstico mostrou que PMAX atraiu buscas "how to" e "diy" que não convertem. Em vez de depender só de negative keywords, crie este segment com bid -50% para reduzir ainda mais o desperdício com curiosos que querem consertar sozinhos.

### Demographic Segments (Observation)

| Segment | Bid Adjustment | Justificativa |
|---|---|---|
| Age 35-54 | +15% | Core boat owner demographic |
| Age 55+ | +10% | Yacht owners, higher disposable income |
| Household Income: Top 10% | +15% | Premium boat owners who don't DIY |
| Homeowners | +10% | Higher correlation with boat ownership |
| Parental Status: Not a parent | +10% | More disposable time for boating |

### Recommended Targeting Rollout

> ⚠️ **Atenção:** No início, deixe TUDO como Observation (não Targeting). O Search com Maximize Clicks já é restritivo pelas keywords. Audience targeting pode cortar volume prematuramente. Use as primeiras 2-4 semanas para coletar dados de qual segmento converte, depois aplique bid adjustments e, só então, migre para Targeting.

| Fase | Ação |
|---|---|
| Semana 1-2 | Observation apenas — colete dados |
| Semana 3-4 | Aplique bid adjustments (+10% a +20%) nos que mostram CTR > 2% |
| Semana 5+ | Migre para Targeting os segmentos com CPA abaixo da média |
