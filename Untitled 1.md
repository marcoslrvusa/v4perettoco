The US industrial equipment market is undergoing a **structural shift**: nonresidential construction (data centers, manufacturing plants, energy infrastructure) is surging while residential moderates. This bifurcation directly impacts Atlas Copco's two divisions differently:

- **APE (Portable Air):** Tied to **construction activity, rental demand, and manufacturing expansion**. Sweet spot is the **Sun Belt + Industrial Midwest** — states with high nonresidential construction starts, large rental equipment fleets, and manufacturing reshoring.
  
- **PAL (Power & Light):** Driven by **data center buildout, energy infrastructure, and industrial backup power**. Sweet spot is **data center clusters (Virginia, Texas, California, Ohio, Illinois) + hurricane-prone coastal states (Florida, Gulf Coast)**.
