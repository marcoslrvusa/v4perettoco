Why this plan exists — in 3 facts

1. AI already answers — and Premium is in 0 of those answers

199,000 questions per month are asked to AI in recommendation format (“best office cleaning services…”, “best hotel housekeeping…”). Each answer cites 4–14 brands. Premium Cleaning NJ appears in zero — 0% visibility, 0% share of voice.

2. On Google, only the homepage exists — and it doesn't sell

The site ranks only its homepage, for 6 branded keywords, at positions 86–107, with zero organic traffic. There is no service × location page — exactly the format Google and AI cite.

3. The window is open but narrowing

Jani-King (40%) and Coverall (30%) already own AI answers. Every month at 0% strengthens whoever is already cited. The move must be aggressive and within 30 days — not business as usual.
