About Us

Building Newark's Future Since 1988

Founded in 1988 by Diniz Teixeira, Premium Contractors LLC has grown from a modest framing crew into one of New Jersey's most respected commercial construction firms. For over three decades, we've been the backbone behind some of the region's most recognizable hotels, restaurants, and aviation facilities — delivering quality, precision, and reliability on every project.
