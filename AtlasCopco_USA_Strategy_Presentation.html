!

Adding just ~40 negative keywords across both divisions could save 15–20% of the budget immediately. Terms like "parts," "repair," "manual," "rental," "home use," and competitor brand names (when not doing conquest) are pure waste.

Campaign Challenges

4 reactivation hurdles.
Each one has a plan.

01
Quality Score expired — 3 years inactive
Google Ads Quality Scores decay with inactivity. After 3 years, the account starts from near zero. Initial CPCs will be 30–50% higher for the first 2–4 weeks. Expected and manageable — exact/phrase match focus and relevant ad copy rebuild QS quickly.
02
Obsolete ad formats — ETAs must be replaced
All existing Expanded Text Ads are deprecated. Every reactivated campaign needs new Responsive Search Ads (RSAs) with 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. This is standard procedure and an opportunity to refresh messaging for 2026.
03
Historical keyword waste — competitors and noise
55% of APE search spend went to competitor terms (Sullair, Ingersoll Rand, Doosan). PAL captured residential intent instead of industrial buyers. Adding ~40 negative keywords saves 15–20% of budget immediately. Exact and Phrase match only — no Broad.
04
Limited budget — $1,500/division/month
At $50/day per division, every dollar must work. No room for fragmented experiments. One Core Search campaign per division with 4 Ad Groups maximizes traction. PMax is a future consideration for S3+ — not part of the initial structure.
Four challenges to address. 1) Expired Quality Score after 3 years — higher CPCs for 2-4 weeks, manageable. 2) Old ad formats must be replaced with RSAs. 3) Historical keyword waste — 55% went to competitors for APE. 40 negative keywords save 15-20% of budget. 4) Limited budget — $1,500/division/month.
