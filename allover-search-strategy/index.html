Calls and sales are down because the summer surge is over. Most Houston roofing work is built between April and August — hail claims get filed in spring, approvals land weeks later, and roofs get built in summer. By September, that pipeline empties. Broad or mixed-intent campaigns keep spending on the same expensive clicks (roofing near me at $22.44, roofing companies near me at $24.07) while high-urgency demand — leaks after heat + storms, replacements clearing deductibles, pre-fall installs — goes to whoever shows up first with the right message.

The fix is not more budget on the same structure. It is three separated Search campaigns by job economics — Repair for speed and call volume, Replacement for revenue, Install for margin and commercial — each with its own ad groups, landing pages, negatives, and bid targets. Your Q4_25 keyword base already proves the split: Repair holds the highest transactional volume (roofing repair near me 6,600/mo), Replacement holds the highest value per click (roofing replacement near me at $31.58 CPC), and Install hides the most waste (DIY how to install metal roofing 3,600/mo must be excluded).

September is the best possible moment to do this. Houston sits in peak hurricane season (Aug–Sep) with a stable fall booking window (Sep–Nov) right after it. Homeowners who deferred through summer want predictable scheduling before the next storm; commercial owners worry about drainage and ponding liability. A "Free Storm Inspection + Fix-Before-Fall" Search program capturing that intent recovers calls in week 1–2 and converts them into replacements in week 3–6 — exactly the cash-flow bridge Q4 needs.

Why calls dropped — the 3 bottlenecks (TOC view)

1. One bucket for three different jobs

Repair ($500–$2,500, decides in hours), Replacement ($8,500–$18,200, decides in weeks, insurance-driven), and Install/Metal ($18,500–$38,400, decides in months) were competing for the same budget and the same ad. Google fed the cheapest clicks — often DIY or supply intent like metal roofing panels ($2.24, 12,100 vol, zero job intent) — while $28–$42 emergency and replacement clicks lost impression share.

2. September intent shifted, message did not

Summer copy around hail season no longer matches September psychology: post-heat leaks (130–150°F attic temps + granule loss), hurricane anxiety, and "schedule it cleanly in fall" convenience. Generic best roofing company ads lose to competitors running Emergency Repair — Same-Day Tarping and Free Inspection + Insurance Help.

3. No call-first plumbing for a call-first business

Roofing in Houston closes on the phone, not the form. Without call assets, call-only logic on mobile for emergency terms, call tracking, 24/7 coverage signals, and speed-to-lead under 5 minutes, even good clicks die. This plan fixes tracking before scaling spend.

01 — Why September in Houston Changes Everything

Houston is not a generic roofing market. Four simultaneous stressors — 130–150°F attic heat, 75%+ year-round humidity, Texas hail leadership (902 major hail events statewide in 2025 alone), and Gulf hurricane exposure — compress roof life to 15–22 years for asphalt instead of the 25–30-year warranty. September sits at the intersection of all four.

04 — Campaign Architecture (Search only, Houston-first)

Three money campaigns + one brand-defense campaign. No mixing. Each campaign has its own budget, landing page, and CPA target — because a $900 repair and a $15,000 replacement can never share a bid strategy. Geo: Harris + Fort Bend + Brazoria + Galveston + Montgomery counties, presence-based, with suburb bid modifiers (Katy, Cypress, Tomball, Spring, Pearland, Sugar Land, The Woodlands). Ad schedule: 6am–11pm with +30% mobile 4–9pm + weekends for repair/emergency; replacement/install full-day with call-handling coverage.

C-1 · REPAIR — "Stop the Leak Today" (45% of budget)
Call volume
Goal: Recover calls in 7–14 days. Cheapest qualified clicks, fastest close. Every repair visit is also a replacement inspection.
Ad groups (exact + phrase only): (1) General Repair — [roofing repair near me], "roofing repair houston"; (2) Emergency — [emergency roofing repair near me], "emergency roofing repair"; (3) Leak — [roof leak repair], "roofing leaks repair"; (4) Shingle — [roofing shingle repair]; (5) Storm — "roofing storm damage repair" (weather-triggered); (6) Commercial Repair — [commercial roofing repair near me] isolated with higher tCPA.
Bidding: Start Maximize Conversions (call + form, 2 weeks, no cap) → move to tCPA $150–$190 once 30 conversions in 30 days. Emergency group: Target Impression Share 75% top + call-only test on mobile.
Landing: /roof-repair-houston/ — sticky mobile call button, "Same-day inspection + tarping if needed," leak-photo upload, ZIP checker, 50+ Houston reviews above fold.
C-2 · REPLACEMENT — "Own the $15k Job" (35% of budget)
Revenue
Goal: Fill the fall install calendar. Fewer clicks, 6–10x ticket vs repair. Protect impression share on the two hero terms at all costs.
Ad groups: (1) Replacement Core — [roofing replacement near me], "roofing replacement houston"; (2) Company — [roofing replacement company], "roofing replacement companies"; (3) Residential — [residential roofing replacement]; (4) Commercial Replacement — [commercial roofing replacement] separate budget cap.
Bidding: Maximize Conversion Value with value rules (replacement form = $12,800 proxy, repair upsell = $1,400) → tCPA $240–$320. Never let Install or broad terms steal this budget.
Landing: /roof-replacement-houston/ — cost-range transparency ($8.5k–$18.2k arch. shingle), deductible explainer, financing calculator, Class 4 upgrade section, before/after Katy + Cypress proof.
C-3 · INSTALL — "Premium & Commercial Pipeline" (15% of budget)
Margin
Goal: Capture metal + new-system + commercial intent without letting DIY burn spend. Exact-heavy, negative-heavy.
Ad groups: (1) Install Core — [roofing installation near me], [roofing installation companies near me]; (2) Metal — [metal roofing installation contractors], [metal roofing installers near me]; (3) Commercial Install — [commercial roofing installation] (TPO/flat copy).
Bidding: tCPA $200–$280 from day one (small, controlled). Phrase only where Search Term history is clean; otherwise exact.
Landing: /new-roof-installation-houston/ + /metal-roofing-houston/ — lifecycle-cost comparison (1 metal vs 2 asphalt cycles), HOA compliance, TPO section for PMs.
C-0 · BRAND DEFENSE (5% of budget, always on)
Protection
Why: Competitors and aggregators (Angi, HomeAdvisor) bid on contractor names in Houston. $1–$3 CPCs to protect your highest-converting traffic.
Build: [all over exterior roofing], [all over roofing houston], [allovertxroofing] + misspellings. Target IS 95%+. Sitelinks to Repair / Replacement / Reviews / Financing.

  • Sticky mobile call bar on every page + click-to-call in headline. 60%+ of repair conversions are calls.
